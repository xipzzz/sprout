## Top 8 for Sprout Scan→Duo-style Lock B

1. **Which of these is X? / text or image MC** — already Lock B SoT (Duo forensic 2026-09-16).  
2. **Select the missing word (cloze MC)** — worksheet blanks with choices.  
3. **Tap / select matching pairs** — word↔def or word↔picture when Scan finds pairs.  
4. **Word-bank / arrange the words** — unscramble & sentence build.  
5. **True/False or “which sentence is correct?”** — judge grammar from homework.  
6. **Grammar / form selection** — have/has, tense, agreement (common Scan stems).  
7. **Listen & select (MC)** — optional TTS on stem; no mic.  
8. **Type (or tile) the blank / flashcard recall** — spelling & vocab from scanned word lists.
